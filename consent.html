Elara SMS consent (opt-in)

This is a private, two-way informational SMS program between the single Elara account holder and Elara. It is not a public service, marketing list, or blast campaign.

This public page records consent only. It does not send SMS.

Who is messaged

  • Recipient: the Elara account holder (the person who owns the software account).
  • No guests, customers, employees, or third-party lists are messaged.
  • Messages may include requested replies and occasional Elara-initiated check-ins or reminders only when that capability is supported and enabled.

Web Form opt-in

This is the only opt-in method. Enter the account-holder number, read the disclosure, check the box, and submit. The server records the phone, server timestamp, exact disclosure/version, and source before this page reports success.

The SMS consent box above loads unchecked. Submit stays blocked until it is checked. Checking it is the affirmative opt-in action.

Program disclosures

  • Message type: private, two-way informational SMS between the account holder and Elara.
  • Frequency: varies with requested replies and any supported, enabled check-ins or reminders.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help.
  • No SMS keyword opt-in (do not text JOIN/START to opt in).
  • Privacy: privacy.html. Terms: terms.html.
