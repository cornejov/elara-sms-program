Elara SMS terms (Sole Proprietor)

This program sends informational application-to-person SMS from Elara software (operated by a Sole Proprietor doing business as Elara) to the account holder after explicit SMS consent. It is not marketing, not mass outreach, and not person-to-person consumer chat.

The account holder opts in on the public consent page by checking an unchecked-by-default SMS box, or via the matching in-product checkbox documented on that page. Consent is optional and is not a condition of using Elara software.

By opting in, the recipient agrees to receive service notifications, replies, and reminders about their Elara software. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
