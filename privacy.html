Elara SMS privacy (Sole Proprietor)

This SMS program is operated by the Sole Proprietor doing business as Elara, a personal technology software product. The only recipient is the account holder, who opts in on the public consent page (Web Form) and/or via the in-product SMS checkbox documented on that page (Other). This page is public and does not require a login.

We collect the account holder’s mobile number and the fact of SMS consent so Elara software can send informational messages the recipient agreed to receive (service notifications, replies, and reminders). Numbers are used only to deliver those consented messages.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are not rented or added to any third-party list.

Message frequency varies with software events. This is not a marketing blast. Unprompted messages are limited. Message and data rates may apply.
