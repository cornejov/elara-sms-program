Elara SMS privacy (Sole Proprietor)

This private SMS program is operated by a Sole Proprietor. The only recipient is the single account holder, who may opt in only on the public consent page. That page shows a carrier-review sample of the opt-in form; live SMS consent is recorded only after a private one-use invitation.

The consent record contains the account-holder mobile number, a server-generated timestamp, the exact disclosure and version accepted, the consent source, and later opt-out/help events. It is kept in the operator-controlled private consent store, not this public repository.

The number is used only for private, two-way informational messages the account holder consented to receive: requested replies and occasional Elara-initiated check-ins or reminders only when supported and enabled.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are not rented or added to any third-party list.

Consent records are retained as needed to prove consent and honor opt-out. Message frequency varies. This is not a marketing blast. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP records a controlling opt-out in Elara’s consent ledger and invalidates send authorization. Reply START does not restore Elara send authorization (Twilio delivery-only). After STOP, a new private one-use invitation web consent is required before any further outbound.
